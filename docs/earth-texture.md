# Earth texture

`public/assets/earth-daymap.jpg` is the Earth atmosphere/day map from the official Three.js examples:
https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg

The Three.js project is MIT licensed: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE
The scene, paper-plane geometry, flight route and composer are original Webstell implementation, inspired by the publicly visible Aceternity Paper Plane Globe interaction. No Aceternity source or assets are included.
